# Whole site (Persuade)

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an entrance at golden hour: real photographs of finished signs, handled like
prints, with gilded lettering. It refuses the sign-shop template and the flat minimal page it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: warm white lit by champagne and leaf-green light; forest green ink; a metallic gold
gradient that only gilds; heavy Besley; pills, soft green-tinted shadows, arched photographs on
gilded base lines, sky-and-lawn scenes for illustrated signs.

STORY: the visitor sees finished signs at once, reads that monuments are all this shop does, sees
five steps and that nothing is final, sees prices to scale, and sends a few words.

FIRST VIEWPORT: headline left with two actions and three facts; right, a fanned deck of photograph
prints that deals itself, with a name plate and previous and next buttons.

FORM: photographic entrance with a print deck. seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
