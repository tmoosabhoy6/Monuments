What a routed HDU monument is

HDU is high-density urethane: a dense board made for outdoor signs.

It has no grain and takes up almost no water, so it carves cleanly and holds its shape outdoors. Your wording and logo are routed into the face, so the lettering stands in relief, and the whole sign is painted in your colors.

Single-sided or double-sided

It depends on how people approach the sign. Tell us about the spot and we will suggest one.

  • Single-sided

    For a spot where everyone approaches from one direction, or where the sign sits against a wall, a fence or planting.

  • Double-sided

    Stands at a right angle to the road, so it reads from both directions.

Four shapes for the top
