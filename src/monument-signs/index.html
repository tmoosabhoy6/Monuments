What a monument sign is

A monument sign stands at ground level on its own base or posts, usually where people turn into a property. Because it sits low and close to the road, it reads at driving speed and frames the entrance rather than towering over it. It is the most permanent sign a property has, and often the first impression of everything behind it.

Ours are routed from HDU, high-density urethane: a router carves the lettering, border and logo in relief, so they stand proud of the background and cast real shadows through the day. Then the sign is primed and painted in your colors.

Monument sign styles

1. Arched face on a stone-look base

The style most people picture: a gently arched face with a raised border, standing on a stone-look base with a cap. It suits neighborhoods, churches and campuses, and it works at every one of our standard sizes.

  • {{> icon-check}}Stone-look base in three staggered courses

2. Pillars with caps

A pillar at each side frames the sign and gives an entrance more presence. Pillars stand taller than the face and finish with a flat or peaked cap. They match the base: stone-look with a stone-look base, smooth with a plain one.

3. On posts

The face hangs between two posts with small caps, set in the ground. It is a lighter look for parks, smaller businesses and secondary entrances, and the posts can be painted to match the sign.

4. Shouldered and peaked tops

A shouldered top raises the center with stepped corners; a peaked top rises to a point. Both give a more formal outline than an arch and pair well with traditional architecture.

5. Cut metal letters

Individual letters cut from metal and mounted on the face, standing off it so they cast a crisp shadow. A strong, modern choice for business parks and commercial entrances.

6. Cut acrylic letters

Individual letters cut from acrylic and mounted on the face. Clean edges and even color, in any of our paints.

7. Double-sided signs

A single-sided sign suits a spot where everyone approaches from one direction or the sign sits against a backdrop. A double-sided sign stands at a right angle to the road so it reads from both directions. Both sides carry the same design.

Illuminated monument signs

We also build illuminated monument signs: lit faces with push-through or reverse-cut lettering, channel letters and face-lit letters. Ask for lighting in the designer or the quote form and we price it with your drawing.

How a routed sign is made

A router carves your lettering, border and logo in relief from a solid HDU board. The raised parts catch the light; the recessed background sits in their shadow. Every sign is made for its order; nothing comes off a shelf.
