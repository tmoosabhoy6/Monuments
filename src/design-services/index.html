How design works

Design starts once your deposit is paid, and it ends when you say it does.

  1. 1

    Send what you have

    Your logo as a PDF if you have one. If not, send the text and we design it for you.

  2. 2

    See the visuals

    Your designer sends visuals of your sign. You won’t know what is perfect until you see them.

  3. 3

    Change anything

    Size, shape, colors, wording. Revisions are free, as many as it takes.

  4. 4

    Approve the proof

    When you are 100% happy, you approve the proof, and we build exactly that.
