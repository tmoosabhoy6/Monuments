One company, from the first drawing to the sign in the ground.

Most sign shops make a monument sign now and then. We make them every day, and we make nothing else. That focus is why we can publish our sizes and prices, show you your sign before anything is built, and send a certified installer to set it permanently, wherever you are in the country.

Our signs stand at the entrances of subdivisions and apartment communities, churches, schools, parks, business parks and individual businesses.

  1. 1Design it

    There are three ways to start. Pick a standard size, design your own in the builder, or describe something the builder cannot draw. All three lead to the same next step: a drawing of your sign.

    Open the builderSee standard sizes

    What we need from you

    Your name as it should read on the sign. Your logo, if it goes on the sign: a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG) is best, and a clear JPG or PNG is enough to start. Your colors, as paint codes if you have them. The ZIP code where the sign will stand.

    Matching your colors

    Give us a Pantone, Sherwin-Williams or other paint code, or send a sample, and we match it. Sign paint is opaque and printing ink is not, so a match is very close rather than identical, and color always looks a little different on a screen. Your approval drawing names the exact paints we will use.

  2. 2Approve the drawing

    Before anything is built, we send you a scaled drawing of your sign: its exact dimensions, its lettering and colors, and how it stands. Ask for changes until it is right. Production starts only after you approve it in writing.

    Taking it to a board or committee

    Most of our signs are approved by a group. Every design you make here has a link you can send and a sheet you can print, so everyone is looking at the same sign, the same options and the same price.

    Checking your site

    Tell us where the sign will go and send a few photos of the spot with something in the picture for scale. We will ask about the ground, anything buried nearby, and how far the sign sits from the road.

  4. 4We build it

    Your sign is routed from HDU, high-density urethane: a dense board made for outdoor signs. A router carves your lettering, border and logo in relief, so they stand proud of the background and cast real shadows through the day. Then it is primed and painted in your colors. Every sign is made for its order; nothing comes off a shelf. Production takes {{lead_time}} from the day you approve your drawing.

    {{@anatomy}}

    We also build illuminated monument signs: lit faces with push-through or reverse-cut lettering, channel letters and face-lit letters. Ask for lighting in the builder or the quote form and we price it with your drawing.

    Why HDU

    Unlike wood, HDU has no grain and takes up almost no water, which is why it holds a crisp carved edge outdoors. It routes cleanly at any depth, so fine lettering and detailed logos come out sharp, and it takes paint evenly on every surface.

  5. 5We install it

    We install permanently, anywhere in the United States, through our network of certified installers. Your installer sets the sign, levels it and leaves it finished. You deal with one company from the first drawing to the sign in the ground.

    Before installation day

    We agree the exact position with you in advance. Underground lines are marked before anyone digs, through the free 811 service. Clear access to the spot is all we need from you on the day.

    How the sign stands

    Depending on the design, your sign stands on posts set in the ground or on a base. Your approval drawing shows which, and what goes below ground.
