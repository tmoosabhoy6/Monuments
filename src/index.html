What is a monument sign?

A monument sign is a low, freestanding sign set permanently at ground level, usually at an entrance where people turn in. It carries a name, sometimes an address or a logo, and it is the first thing a visitor sees of a neighborhood, a church, a school or a business.

Most sign shops make one now and then. We make them every day and nothing else, which is why we can publish our sizes and prices, show you your sign before anything is built, and send a certified installer to set it, wherever you are in the country.

Why HDU

The material made for carved signs

High-density urethane has no grain and takes up almost no water, so it holds a crisp carved edge outdoors. It routes cleanly at any depth, so fine lettering and detailed logos come out sharp, and it takes paint evenly on every surface.
