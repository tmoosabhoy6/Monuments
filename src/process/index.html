The five steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Tell us what you want on the sign

    Single-sided or double-sided. Send your logo as a PDF, or send the text and we will design it for you.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Give us an approximate size

    It does not need to be exact, just a starting point. Your sign can change during the proposal phase and the design phase with the designer, at no charge.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Send us your contact info

    So we can send your proposal and reach you with questions.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Small deposit, then design

    After the deposit is paid, we create your design and work with you until you are 100% happy. If samples are needed, we can send samples of your project.

    Step 5: Proof approval, production and delivery
